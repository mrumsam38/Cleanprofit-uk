Data processing information

Updated 18 November 2026

Service status: CleanProfit UK is currently in testing and is not accepting live subscriptions. Final business identity, contact details and commercial terms will be provided before paid launch.

CleanProfit UK helps cleaning businesses store and manage records about their own customers. This page explains the intended data protection responsibilities for that information.

Who is responsible for customer data?

The cleaning business normally decides which customer details to collect and why. It therefore generally acts as the data controller. CleanProfit UK provides the application and ordinarily acts as a data processor for records stored on the business's behalf.

What records may be processed?

Names, contact details, service addresses, job descriptions, appointment information, quotes, invoices and payment records entered by subscribers. Please do not enter unnecessary sensitive personal information into free-text fields.

How records are used

Information is stored, retrieved, updated and protected to provide the application. Account and billing information may be handled separately by the service operator for its own purposes, as described in the privacy policy.

Security and service providers

Supabase provides authentication and cloud database services. The application uses sign-in and database access restrictions. Details of subprocessors, data transfers, security commitments and deletion procedures will be included in the final data processing agreement.

Data processing agreement

A formal agreement covering documented instructions, confidentiality, security, subprocessors, assistance with data subject requests, breach notification, return or deletion of records and applicable audit rights is being prepared. This information page is not itself a signed or binding data processing agreement.

Your responsibilities

As a cleaning business, you remain responsible for giving your customers appropriate privacy information, handling their rights requests and using personal information lawfully.
