Privacy information

Pre-launch draft — not final legal terms. CleanProfit UK Ltd is not yet registered. This page is published for transparency during testing and will be replaced with approved information before live subscriptions begin.

Who is responsible for your information?

CleanProfit UK is a proposed subscription software service for UK cleaning businesses. The proposed limited company has not yet been incorporated. Its final legal name, registered address and privacy contact will be provided before commercial launch.

Information used by the service

Account email addresses and authentication information; cleaning-business customer details, contact information, addresses, quotes, jobs, appointments, invoices and recorded payments entered by subscribers; and subscription status and billing identifiers.

Why it is used

To provide account access, save and display business records, manage subscriptions, maintain security and respond to service enquiries. Specific lawful bases and retention periods are being finalised for launch.

Service providers

The current test service uses Supabase for authentication and cloud data, Stripe for subscription payments in Sandbox, and GitHub Pages for hosting. Provider data-processing terms, locations and international transfer arrangements require confirmation before launch.

Cleaning businesses' own customers

Subscribers are generally responsible for deciding what personal information about their customers they enter and for giving those customers appropriate privacy information. Processor terms are being prepared.

Your rights

UK data-protection law may provide rights of access, correction, deletion, objection and other rights depending on the circumstances. You can learn about these rights or raise concerns with the Information Commissioner's Office. A working privacy contact will be published before launch.

Cookies and local storage

The application uses browser storage for account sessions and application behaviour. A detailed storage and cookie inventory is being finalised.
