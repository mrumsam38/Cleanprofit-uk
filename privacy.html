Privacy policy

Updated 19 November 2026

This notice explains how CleanProfit UK handles personal information when you use the website and cleaning-business management application.

Who handles your data

CleanProfit UK operates the cleaning-business management service. An application to incorporate CleanProfit UK Ltd (company number supplied: 17591388; incorporation confirmation pending) has been submitted and is awaiting confirmation. The legal operator details and a direct privacy contact will be updated once confirmed.

Information we use

  • Account details, including email address, account identifiers and authentication information.
  • Subscription status and billing identifiers, including information received from our payment provider.
  • Business records you enter, such as customer names, contact details, service addresses, cleaning jobs, quotations, invoices, appointments and payment records.
  • Technical and security information generated through the hosting and authentication services.

How we use information

We use account information to provide access to the application, store and retrieve your records, manage subscriptions, respond to support requests and protect the service against misuse. Depending on the activity and whether the account holder is an individual or company, the relevant lawful basis may be performance of a contract, legitimate interests or compliance with legal obligations.

Your customers' information

Cleaning businesses generally decide why and how they process information about their own customers. In those circumstances, the cleaning business is the data controller and CleanProfit UK acts as a service provider processing information on its behalf. See our data processing information.

Our service providers

We use Supabase for account authentication and cloud records, Stripe for live subscription billing, Resend for transactional account emails, and GitHub Pages for website hosting. These providers may process technical or personal information as part of delivering their services. These providers may process data in the UK or overseas according to their applicable service terms and transfer safeguards.

How long information is kept

Information is kept as needed to provide the service and meet applicable legal, security and accounting requirements. Cancelling a subscription does not automatically erase stored records. Contact the service operator to request account closure or deletion; some information may need to be retained for legal, security or accounting reasons. Do not assume that cancelling a subscription automatically deletes stored records.

Security

Account authentication and database access controls are used to protect records. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Use a strong, unique password and keep your sign-in details private.

Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives individuals rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the use of personal information and, in some cases, to data portability. For information entered by a cleaning business about its own customers, please contact that business first. You may also contact the Information Commissioner's Office.

Cookies and device storage

The application uses browser storage and authentication session information needed for sign-in and application operation. See our cookies and browser storage notice for more information.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service develops. Significant changes will be communicated where required by law.
