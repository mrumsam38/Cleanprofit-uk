Cookies and browser storage

Updated 18 November 2026

We use browser storage and authentication technologies to run CleanProfit UK. This page explains their purposes and the choices available to you.

Essential account storage

Supabase authentication uses browser storage to keep you signed in and maintain your session. Disabling this storage may prevent sign-in or make the application unusable.

Application storage

The application also uses local browser storage for working data, settings and preserved recovery copies. Information stored on a device may remain until cleared by you or the application. Clearing browser data can remove local copies that have not been saved elsewhere.

Payment services

If you choose to open Stripe Checkout or the Stripe customer billing portal, Stripe may use cookies and related technologies to process the transaction and prevent fraud. Stripe's own privacy information applies to its payment pages.

Website hosting

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Hosting infrastructure may process network and security logs when pages are requested.

Analytics and advertising

We have not identified any separate advertising or analytics cookies in the current application source review. This does not establish a complete audit of all third-party technologies; we will update this notice if additional services are introduced.

Your choices

You can manage or clear site storage in your browser settings. Blocking essential authentication storage can stop the service working. Where non-essential cookies requiring consent are introduced, appropriate consent controls must be provided before they are used.

More information

Read our privacy policy for information about personal data and your rights.
