Terms of use

Pre-launch draft — not final legal terms. CleanProfit UK Ltd is not yet registered. This page is published for transparency during testing and will be replaced with approved information before live subscriptions begin.

Intended users

CleanProfit UK is being developed for cleaning businesses. These terms are a pre-launch summary, not a final customer agreement. The intended service operator, legal address and company number will be confirmed following incorporation.

What the software does

The software helps users manage cleaning customers, calculate estimates, create quotations, schedule visits, issue invoices and record payments. Estimates depend on information entered by the user and are not tax, accounting or legal advice.

Your responsibilities

Users must keep account credentials secure, enter customer information lawfully, verify their invoices and pricing, and avoid attempts to access other users' records.

Service limitations

The current release candidate supports cloud saving when connected. Offline editing and simultaneous edits on multiple devices are not supported. It should not be treated as a guaranteed archival backup.

Subscriptions and cancellations

Proposed price: £9.99 per month, with a proposed seven-day trial. The final VAT treatment, trial rules, cancellation timing and refunds policy will be displayed before any live subscription can be purchased. Current checkout is a Stripe test environment and does not take real payments.

Data protection

Subscribers remain responsible for their customers' data. Final privacy information and a data processing agreement will be available before commercial launch.

Contact

A monitored support email and full legal contact details will be provided before launch.
