Subscription and billing

Updated 19 November 2026

Information about CleanProfit UK's live monthly subscription.

Monthly subscription

£9.99 per month is the advertised subscription price. The final amount payable, including any applicable taxes, is displayed before payment in Stripe Checkout.

Free trial

New accounts receive a seven-day free trial with no payment card required. The trial does not automatically convert to a paid subscription. To continue with paid access, choose to subscribe. If you subscribe during the trial, Stripe may charge you immediately.

Renewal

Paid subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled. Your billing date and amount are shown in Stripe Checkout and your billing portal. If a payment fails, subscription access may be restricted. We will give notice of material pricing changes as required by law.

Cancellation

You can manage and cancel a paid subscription through the Stripe customer billing portal in your account. Cancellation prevents future renewals; access generally remains available until the current paid period ends. Refund requests are assessed under applicable law and the circumstances of the charge; cancellation does not itself issue a refund.

Live payments

Subscription payments are processed securely by Stripe in live mode. CleanProfit UK does not ask you to enter card details directly into the application.

Before subscribing

Review our terms of service and privacy policy. The service operator's legal identity and contact information must also be provided as part of the published service information.
