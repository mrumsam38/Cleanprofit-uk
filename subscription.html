Subscription and billing

Updated 18 November 2026

Service status: CleanProfit UK is currently in testing and is not accepting live subscriptions. Final business identity, contact details and commercial terms will be provided before paid launch.

Clear information about the proposed CleanProfit UK subscription.

Monthly subscription

£9.99 per month is the planned subscription price. Whether VAT is included or added has not yet been confirmed.

Free trial

A seven-day free trial is planned. Whether a payment card is required, when the first charge occurs and whether any reminder is issued will be confirmed before the live checkout is enabled.

Renewal

The intended subscription renews monthly until cancelled. Final billing dates, failed-payment handling and any price-change notice will be explained before payment.

Cancellation

The Stripe billing portal is intended to provide subscription management and cancellation. Test cancellation has been observed to preserve access until the paid period ends. The final cancellation and refund rules will be provided before live checkout.

Payments during testing

The current application uses Stripe test mode. No real subscription payments are accepted through this version. Do not enter a real payment card into a test checkout.

Before subscribing

Review our terms of service and privacy policy. Full business contact information and the final subscription agreement will be available before commercial launch.
