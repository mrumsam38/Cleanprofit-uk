## 2. Information we process
- **Account details:** email address, authentication credentials managed by our authentication provider, account identifiers and subscription status.
- **Business information entered by users:** customer names, contact details, addresses, cleaning jobs, quotes, invoices, appointments, payments recorded, and pricing/settings information.
- **Subscription information:** subscription and billing status and identifiers provided by our payment provider. We do not intend to store full payment-card numbers in the application.
- **Technical information:** security, diagnostic and service-access data processed by hosting, authentication and payment providers, as applicable.

**Important distinction:** Cleaning businesses may enter information about *their own customers*. For that information, the cleaning business is generally the controller and we provide software as a processor, subject to an appropriate data processing agreement.
